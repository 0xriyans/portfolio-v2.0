Backend framework migration at Prudential replacing Apache Flink with lightweight **Quarkus** services, cutting container memory from over **1.5 GB to under 300 MB** (**60%+ reduction**). Transformed fund withdrawals from **2 business days to real-time** with zero-loss **Apache Kafka** manual offset commit and a **PostgreSQL ACID ledger**.
